Section 3 · Qualification stakes

Knockout football: no dead rubbers, no games to throw

How they got here

France won Group I (Norway, Senegal, Iraq) with a perfect 9 points and +8 goal difference, then dismissed Sweden 3–0 and ground out 1–0 v Paraguay in 100°F heat in Philadelphia.

Morocco finished level on 7 points with Brazil in Group C, edged out on goal difference only, then survived the Netherlands on penalties and dominated Canada 3–0.

Motivation read

Single elimination — no incentive whatsoever to draw or lose, and no goal-difference games. A draw after 90' sends it to extra time (bets here settle at 90').

Winner meets Spain or Belgium in the semi-final. Morocco's rational path is exactly what they did v Brazil and Netherlands: stay compact, keep it 0–0 deep into the game, and take their chances late or on penalties. France want an early goal to avoid that script — precisely how they solved this fixture in 2022.

Section 5 · Match prediction

Best call: France 2–0 — and a game shorter on goals than the market thinks

Most likely scoreline

France 2 – 0 Morocco

1–0 is fractionally more likely cell-by-cell (22% v 17% of the top-8 mass); 2–0 is chosen as the headline call because France's pattern v deep blocks — and the 2022 semi itself — is one goal in normal flow, one late against a stretched, chasing side.

Model: independent-Poisson, France xG 1.60, Morocco xG 0.60 (total 2.2 v the market's ~2.5). Calibration check: the model reproduces the market's 1X2 almost exactly (France 62% / Draw 25% / Morocco 14% v de-vigged 61 / 25 / 15) — the disagreement is only about how many goals, which is where the value is.

Tactical summary

Ouahbi will do to France what he did to Brazil and the Netherlands: a compact 4-2-3-1 mid-block, El Aynaoui and Bouaddi playing through the press, tempo strangled, with Hakimi and Ounahi the outlets — both of those games finished 1–1. Deschamps, missing Tchouaméni's ball-winning, will press slightly less aggressively and trust Olise and Doué to move the block before releasing Mbappé into the left channel — Hakimi v Mbappé is the game inside the game. Add a 4pm July kick-off in East-coast heat (the Paraguay game was played at ~100°F and produced one goal) and everything points to a patient, low-tempo match decided by one or two moments rather than a shoot-out.

Risk assessment — the wildcard

Game state is the whole bet. The single scenario that invalidates this analysis is an early Morocco goal (as in 2022 — but in reverse — when France scored on 5'). If Morocco lead inside 30', France must chase, the block breaks open at both ends, and every under-flavoured position below is in danger simultaneously — the portfolio is deliberately correlated and does not pretend otherwise.

Secondary risks: Saibari passing fit changes Morocco's counter-threat; a red card or early penalty; my 2.2-goal total v the market's 2.5 is a genuine disagreement — if the market is right, these edges shrink to roughly break-even.

Reasoning per bet

1 · Under 2.5 @ 1.88 — the core edge

1xBet implies 53.2%; the model says 62.3% — a +9.1-point edge, the largest on the board. Morocco's elite-opponent games went 1–1 and 1–1; France's knockout games have been 3–0 v a third-place qualifier and a heat-strangled 1–0; the last competitive France–Morocco was 2–0. The market line of ~2.5 goals looks anchored to France's group-stage goal glut against open teams.

2 · Asian under 3.0 @ 1.42 — the safety-first version

Same thesis, one goal of insurance: at exactly 3 goals (e.g. 2–1 late consolation) the stake comes back instead of losing. Win 62%, refund 20%, lose only on 4+ goals (18%). This is the "wider line at acceptable odds" the safety rule demands, so it carries the second-largest stake.

3 · 1X + Under 2.5 @ 2.11 — priced-in insult, small edge on top

France-or-draw is 86% likely on its own; combined with under 2.5 the model gives 53.6% v 47.4% implied (+6.3). It only differs from bet 1 by also losing to a 0–1/0–2 Morocco win (8.6%) — and pays 2.11 instead of 1.88 for that. Smallest stake of the four, matching its lowest confidence.

4 · BTTS + Over 3.5 "No" @ 1.22 — the wide safety net

Loses only if both teams score and the game has 4+ goals (3–1, 2–2, 4–1…) — a 12% scenario in the model v 18% implied (+5.7). Crucially it still wins if France run up 3–0 or 4–0, which is the one France-blowout branch the under bets can't cover, so it de-correlates the portfolio slightly.
